======================================================

For voltage and current plots, read printed plot titles and annotations.

Process activity: active or inactive or uncertain
Spike count: value or unknown

Signal interpretation:
* Stable repeated pattern with low spike and extinction count supports Stable arc.
* Strong residual fluctuation, high spike count, extinction or arc-collapse events, or unstable trend supports Unstable arc.
* Very low current rolling average (below ~100 A) with high extinction count suggests Not Welding or weld stop condition.
* Current ramping from zero indicates arc ignition transition.

======================================================
STEP 2 — WELD STATE CLASSIFICATION
======================================================

Valid labels: Arc Ignition | Welding

Arc Ignition:
Visual: bright circular burst, no developed trailing melt pool
Metadata: low Area, small Width and Height, high circularity
Signal: pre-arc or ignition-like signal behavior, current rising from zero
Hard exclusion: if metadata bbox or centroid is near colorbar, border, or isolated spatter, ignore shape metadata for Weld State.

Welding:
Visual: melt pool visible with arc present; may or may not have a developed tail
Metadata: developed melt pool region with Area, Width, Height showing significant values
Signal: active voltage and current behavior with rolling averages above noise floor

STEP 5 — ARC STABILITY
======================================================

Apply only if Weld State = Welding. Otherwise: N/A.
Valid labels: Stable | Unstable | N/A

Primary evidence: voltage and current signal plots
Fallback if plots unavailable: thermal image and metadata

Stable:
Signal: regular behavior, low spike count, no strong extinction or arc-collapse events
Visual: hotspot compact and reasonably centered, smooth thermal gradient
Metadata: gradient_std < 55 AND hotspot not near pool boundary

Unstable:
Signal: strong spikes, residual fluctuation, extinction or arc-collapse events, unstable trend
Visual: hotspot offset, diffuse, or near or outside pool boundary, irregular thermal distribution
Metadata: gradient_std > 65 OR large hotspot_centroid_distance_px OR visually erratic arc

Priority rule: if signal plots clearly show instability, prioritize signal over metadata.
