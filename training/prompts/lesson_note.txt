LESSONS LEARNED FROM TRAINING
==================================================
The following corrections were identified during training. Apply them carefully when predicting on new frames.

[MELT POOL STABILITY]
- Re-check Melt Pool Stability using Rule 7(ii) and 7(d) to evaluate thermal appearance and metadata support for instability rather than defaulting to Stable.

[SPATTER PARTICLE SIZE]
- Re-evaluate spatter metadata total area according to Rule 4(ii) to correctly classify as Small.
- Follow rule 4(ii) and evaluate validated spatter area metadata to classify Spatter Particle Size as Fine when total_area is 45 or below.

[SPATTER VOLUME]
- Re-evaluate spatter count according to Rule 5(i) to correctly classify as Minimal.
- Follow rule 5(i) and classify Spatter Volume as Moderate when SpatterCount is between 5 and 14.
